Privacy

13 September 2026

Sprout runs on your own computer. It has no server of its own, and its developer receives no data from it.

Google data

With your permission, Sprout reads your Google Calendar, Tasks, Gmail and Drive, and can add calendar events, tasks and mail drafts. It does this only to carry out your own requests, from your computer. It never sends mail.

Storage

What Sprout reads stays on your computer, in the folder you chose. Your sign-in is kept on your computer and removed when you sign out.

Sharing

Sprout does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and carries no analytics or advertising.

Sprout's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Withdrawing access

Sign out in Sprout, or remove Sprout from your Google Account's permissions page.

Contact

Through the support address on Sprout's Google sign-in screen.
